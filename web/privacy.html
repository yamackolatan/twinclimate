Privacy policy

TwinClimate · last updated 6 November 2026

The short version

TwinClimate has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. What you enter stays on your device. To show the weather, the app sends the coordinates of the place you pick to a weather service.

What stays on your device

  • Places you used, your last sport, distance, start time and pace.
  • Saved races and their typical race-day weather.
  • Your comfort profile, your "How did it feel?" answers and packing ticks.

This is stored only in the app's own storage on your device (or in your browser for the website). We cannot see it. Deleting the app (or clearing the site's data) removes it.

What is sent to other services

  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) receives the coordinates and dates you ask about, to return forecasts, past weather and place search results. See Open-Meteo's terms and privacy information.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim (nominatim.openstreetmap.org) receives coordinates to turn them into place names (for "Use my location" and race-matching results). See the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.

Like any internet request, these services see your device's IP address. TwinClimate sends no name, email, account or device identifier.

Location

The app asks for your location only when you tap "Use my location". It is used once, to look up the weather for that spot, and is not stored or shared beyond the requests described above. You can turn location access off at any time in your phone's settings.

Children

TwinClimate does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
