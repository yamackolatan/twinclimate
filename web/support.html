Help & feedback

TwinClimate tells you what a training session will feel like and what to wear and bring.

Common questions

  • Where does the weather come from? Open-Meteo, for the exact coordinates of your spot. More than 16 days ahead, the app shows typical weather from the last 10 years instead, clearly labelled.
  • "Use my location" doesn't work. Allow location access for TwinClimate in your phone's Settings (or for this site in your browser), or search for the place instead.
  • The kit feels too warm or too cold for me. Answer the short "How did it feel?" question after a session, or set it yourself under Comfort profile. The app learns from both.
  • "The weather service is busy". The free weather service limits requests; wait a minute and try again.
